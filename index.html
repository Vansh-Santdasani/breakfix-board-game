The theme

Innovation Through Years

Each side of the board is one era of HPE ProLiant. As players move clockwise they travel from 2017 to 2025, and every ProLiant tile is a chassis they can build on.

Our people

Players from Finance, HR, Sales, Engineering and every other domain meet at one table. The Hall of Uptime ranks domains too.

Our culture

Nobody is knocked out. A broke player gets a bailout, and the winner is announced with a Hall of Uptime entry for the whole team.

Collaboration

Team Huddles pay the helper. Trades are always open. Six Innovation Stars as a table fill the We Are HPE meter and give every player 200 Cr.

Rules

Play in 60 seconds

Set up

  • Each player starts with 1,500 Cr (2 x 500, 3 x 100, 4 x 50) and a pawn on POWER ON.
  • One person is the Dispatcher: holds the bank and runs the Incident Console.
  • Start the Sprint Clock (10 minutes).

Your turn

  • Roll two dice, move clockwise, do what the tile says.
  • Doubles: roll again. Three in a row sends you to Reboot Bay.
  • Passing POWER ON pays 200 Cr.

Buy parts

  • Unowned component: buy it at the printed price or skip.
  • Owned by someone else: pay the rent.
  • ProLiant tiles rent 20 / 50 / 100 / 200 for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 owned. iLO and AHS rent is your dice roll x 5 (x 10 if the owner has both).

Incident!

  • Dispatcher draws a card on the Incident Console.
  • Clues cost 20 Cr each into the Coffee Pot.
  • Team Huddle: ask any one person. If they were right, they earn 50 Cr.
  • Right: +150 Cr and an Innovation Star. Wrong: pay 50 Cr into the Pot.

Upgrade

  • Own all 3 parts of a colour: rent doubles.
  • Pay the upgrade cost on the deed and place an Upgrade Chip: rent becomes 4x and you unlock that subsystem's HPE innovation.

We Are HPE meter

  • Mark every Innovation Star on the meter in the Sprint Clock screen.
  • Fill all 6 before time runs out and every player gets +200 Cr.

Corners and bills

  • Coffee Break: take the whole Coffee Pot.
  • Kernel Panic: go to Reboot Bay, no 200 Cr. There, miss a turn or pay 50 Cr.
  • Power Bill and Licence Renewal: pay 100 / 150 Cr into the Pot.
  • Broke? Sell back at half price, then take a one-time 200 Cr bailout.

Finish at the Build Bay

  • When the clock rings, finish the round and walk to the Build Bay.
  • Tick your parts, hit POWER ON, watch the boot, send your score to the Hall of Uptime.
  • Score = cash + part prices + 100 per upgrade + 150 per star + 100 per boot stage + 200 for a full boot.
